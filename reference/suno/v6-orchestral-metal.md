# Suno v6 orchestral metal production playbook

## Arrangement priorities
- Rhythm guitar: define tuning feel, riff articulation, density and how it leaves space for the orchestra. Avoid indiscriminate walls of guitars.
- Bass: explicitly request audible bass notes and low-mid definition, not only low-frequency weight. Differentiate bass guitar from sub-bass.
- Drums: articulate kick attack and bass separation; preserve snare body, tom fills, controlled cymbals and dynamic variation.
- Orchestra: assign functions to strings, brass, choir and piano separately, with handoffs between verses, builds and choruses. Keep strings from masking vocals and guitars.
- Vocals: specify register and intent, nuanced dynamics, intelligible central lead, and restrained doubled harmonies.
- Finale: have one clear payoff and contrasting quieter passage before it.

## Strong starter Style
`Cinematic orchestral progressive metal, driving but emotionally spacious. Precise palm-muted guitars lock with punchy live drums; articulate warm bass guitar remains clearly audible in the low mids, separate from kick drum. Verses feature intimate clean lead vocals over cellos, soft piano and restrained guitars. Pre-choruses build through ascending string counterpoint and tom fills. Choruses open to soaring layered strings, wide rhythm guitars and powerful melodic vocals, never burying the lead or bass. Dynamic bridge drops to piano and cello before a final full-band climax. Controlled low end, natural transients, clear separation, minimal harshness.`

## Repairs
| Heard problem | First prompt revision | Post-render follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bass too quiet | Give bass a melodic role; request clear low-mid note definition | Inspect stems; adjust bass gain/EQ against kick |
| Kick masks bass | Simplify low-frequency layers and request a short punchy kick | Use EQ and sidechain where supported |
| Orchestra lost | Assign strings a counter-melody in quieter sections | Balance stems and arrangement |
| Chorus flat | Contrast sparse verse with chorus melody and added harmonies | Use a targeted section replacement |
| Vocals buried | Specify central articulate lead and reduced choir layering | Adjust vocal stem and instrument balance |

These are creative experiments, not guaranteed v6 control keywords. Preserve lyric wording unless explicitly invited to rewrite.
